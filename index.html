Our Story

BUILT DIFFERENT.
RUN DIFFERENT.

Hunthreads started as a simple idea — what if the best tattoo studio, the sharpest barber, and the most authentic streetwear brand were all under one roof?

We're not a chain. We're not a franchise. We're a crew of artists and barbers who care about the craft. Every client leaves looking and feeling like themselves — just sharper.

The mascot says it all: sitting back, clippers in hand, doing exactly what we love. That energy runs through everything we make, cut, and ink.
